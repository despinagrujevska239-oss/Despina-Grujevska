EVENTIFY

01 — OVERVIEW

About the project

Eventify is a concept for an event discovery platform created to make finding events easier, faster and more enjoyable. Users can explore different categories, discover new experiences and find events that fit their interests.

Role

UX/UI Designer

Project

Mobile App

Tools

Figma, Illustrator

Timeline

Academic Project

02 — THE PROBLEM

Finding the right event shouldn't be difficult.

People often have to search through different websites and social media platforms to find events that match their interests. Information can be scattered and difficult to compare.

03 — THE SOLUTION

One place for every experience.

Eventify brings events into one simple and organized platform. Users can browse categories, discover events, view details and choose experiences they would like to attend.

05 — VISUAL DESIGN

A colorful and energetic identity.
